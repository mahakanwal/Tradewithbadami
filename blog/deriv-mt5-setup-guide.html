Deriv MT5 Setup Guide: Login, Add Symbols and Place Your First Trade

By Waseem Badami Jul 18, 2026 7 min read
  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Platforms

In this article

    Most questions in our WhatsApp group during the first week of a batch are about MT5: "Which server?", "Where is Boom 1000?", "How do I set a stop loss?" This guide answers all of them in order.

    Step 1: Create your Deriv MT5 account

    Log in to Deriv and open the Trader's Hub. In the CFDs section, add a Deriv MT5 account — start with a demo. Choose the account type that includes the markets you want (for synthetic indices this is usually the Standard account). Set an MT5 password.

    Step 2: Note your login and server

    After creating the account, Deriv shows:

    • MT5 login — a number that identifies your trading account.
    • Server — the exact server name for your account.

    Use the server name exactly as shown in your Deriv dashboard. Choosing a different server is the number one reason for "Invalid account" errors.

    Step 3: Install MT5 and log in

    On mobile (Android / iPhone)

    1. Install MetaTrader 5 from the official app store.
    2. Open Settings → New Account / Login to an existing account.
    3. Search "Deriv" and select the server that matches your dashboard.
    4. Enter your MT5 login and MT5 password.

    On desktop or web

    Download MT5 from your Deriv dashboard or use the web terminal link Deriv provides. Go to File → Login to Trade Account and enter the same login, password and server.

    Step 4: Add Boom, Crash and volatility indices

    New accounts show only a few symbols. To add more:

    • Mobile: open Quotes, tap the + icon and search for the name, for example "Crash 1000" or "Volatility 75".
    • Desktop: right-click inside Market Watch and choose Symbols (or Show All), then find and enable the indices you want.

    Step 5: Check the specification

    Before trading any symbol, open its Specification (desktop: right-click → Specification). Note the minimum volume, volume step and contract size. These differ between instruments and directly affect your risk.

    Step 6: Place a trade with SL and TP

    1. Open the chart of the symbol and tap or click New Order.
    2. Enter the volume you calculated from your risk.
    3. Set the Stop Loss and Take Profit prices.
    4. Choose Buy or Sell.

    You can also drag SL and TP lines on the chart after opening the trade. Confirm both are visible before you leave the screen.

    Step 7: Set up your chart properly

    • Use candlesticks and switch between H1, M15 and M5.
    • Draw horizontal lines for key support and resistance.
    • Keep indicators minimal — clean charts make clearer decisions.

    Troubleshooting

    ProblemLikely fix
    Invalid accountCheck the server name and use the MT5 password, not your Deriv password.
    Symbol not foundMake sure your account type includes that market, then search the full name.
    Invalid volumeUse the minimum volume and step shown in the Specification.
    No balance in MT5Transfer funds from your Deriv account to the MT5 account.

    Key Takeaways

    • Start with a demo MT5 account and note the login and server.
    • Use the server name exactly as shown in your Deriv dashboard.
    • Add symbols from Quotes or Market Watch and check each Specification.
    • Never open a trade without a stop loss and take profit.
